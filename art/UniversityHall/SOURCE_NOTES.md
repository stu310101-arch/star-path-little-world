# 大學館 — architecture and curated miniature exhibits

The user selected a dignified university library, replacing the rejected toy-like garden studies. Current editable source: `UniversityHall_Library.blend`. Earlier named .blend files and revision folders are draft studies.

All architecture is original metric geometry. Walnut, stone, leather and paper maps are original deterministic mathematical raster bakes in tools/build_university_hall.py. Real 1024px glTF base colour, roughness and normal images replace the old vertex-only palette. UV repeats are intentional at physical scale and construction seams. No external assets or fonts are embedded.

Runtime labels, collision and lighting remain Godot-owned. Two front bankers lamps and two central brass chandeliers in the atrium provide four practical light positions; the gallery has two reading-lamp light positions. All positions and updated furniture collision boxes are in layout.json, including the glass showcase enclosure. Upper galleries are visual architecture and inaccessible. GLBs are replaced atomically. Source images are packed in the editable Blender library. Blender reference-render lights are independent of the final Godot lighting.

The 30 canonical PNGs explicitly enable mipmaps. The Godot post-import script preserves PBR material parameters, enables anisotropic filtering, and points albedo/normal/roughness textures to the shared PNG resources. The self-contained raw GLBs intentionally embed images; release packs should use imported scene resources and their canonical texture dependencies, not raw GLBs.

The previous white model bug was Godot retaining COLOR_0 but disabling vertex_color_use_as_albedo. The new PBR materials must retain image inputs; the importer must not replace them with a vertex-only material.

Revision 3.1 replaces the room floor stacks entirely: the old foundation top, subfloor, separate tile quads, dark border slabs, brass lines and medallion discs were removed. Each room now has exactly one two-triangle floor with a 2048px base-colour atlas, constant 256px normal/roughness maps, and explicit mipmaps. Only this separate floor mesh loses auto-generated LOD; the architecture retains its normal LODs. No secondary horizontal floor surface remains. The existing Godot floor is collision-only.

Both rooms now have a complete positive-Z entrance wall, inward-facing bookcases and upper gallery. The positive-Z return portals were rotated 180 degrees around their own centers so the door panels and brass handles face the interior. Matching door and entrance-bookcase collision coordinates were updated in layout.json. `patch_library_surfaces.py` records the surgical patch from the prior master; the production generator already contains these fixes for clean rebuilds.

Revision 4 uses `UniversityHall_ExhibitKit.blend`, built by `build_exhibit_kit.py` and its `refine_exhibits.py` / `refine_printer.py` helpers. Eight exhibition furniture silhouettes and 36 independently reusable pieces replace the six repeated complete theme models. Direct GLB children have identity transforms; component geometries are floor rooted and constrained to published bounds. School-specific selection and transforms are curated in data rather than generated by random seeds. The active runtime loads only atrium, gallery, exhibit_kit and feature_book. Legacy theme GLBs remain as draft local files and are excluded from releases.

The close-inspection detail pass adds four-sided architectural window reveals, frames, projecting sills, door surrounds and roof coping; continuous pinnate palm leaves and broadleaf crowns; contoured hillside paths, masonry retaining walls, reeds and waterfront seats; curved plank bridges; a working-vessel hull with bridge glazing, radar, rail stanchions, afterdeck winch and sampling crane; an open workshop section with machine beds and north-light roof trusses; and a theatre with fan seating, aisles and lighting truss. NTUST's dedicated teaching-equipment component depicts FDM and resin 3D printing, not an unsupported claim about industrial robot arms.

The dairy teaching shop is a constructed retail vignette: shiplap backing, braced canopy, raised-panel counter, grouped small bottles on two display tiers, hollow refrigerated cabinet with visible stocked shelves, transparent hinged-door surround and handle, cash drawer and tilted POS terminal. Bottle geometry includes body, rounded shoulder, narrow neck and colored cap; neutral paper labels are material regions on the same surface rather than overlapping cylinders. No fictitious brand or storefront lettering is authored.

Hero forms are artistic miniatures, not measured reproductions. Luce Chapel's four separated ochre shells follow the official university description at https://www.thu.edu.tw/web/about/detail.php?scid=89&sid=189. NTU's Fu Bell follows the red outward-splayed four-member iron structure and circular eight-pier platform documented at https://ntualumnibm.ntu.edu.tw/bm.bimonthly.article/detail/sn/9155. A patinated bronze bell with casting bands replaces the earlier incorrect two-post stone portal. All geometry remains originally authored; no third-party model or photograph is embedded.

Physical survey trays have subdued sage, slate and taupe surfaces and a fine brass edge. Their top is .875m, while the school component root remains .88m. They contain only measuring ticks, not invented maps of campus roads. No hall floor mesh or floor texture changed in this revision.

Hill and terrace reliefs include closed earthen side profiles and bottom caps, using the exact original outline and minimum height. This removes the floating-sheet appearance visible from the close-inspection camera while preserving all published bounds and school placements.
