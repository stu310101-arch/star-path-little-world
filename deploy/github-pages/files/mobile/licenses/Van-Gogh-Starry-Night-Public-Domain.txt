# The Starry Night

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Van_Gogh_-_Starry_Night_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg

Texture: 1280 × 1014 pixel JPEG, downloaded 2026-09-29 from the image's published 1280 px thumbnail. The complete painting is used without stretching or cropping.

The framed painting replaces the training room's diagram. Original painting remains unmodified; Blender UVs and game inspection preserve its aspect ratio.
