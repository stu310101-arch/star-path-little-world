# Red-roof homes

Derived from the user's supplied Kenney Suburban pack (`../suburban`, original license retained there).
`tools/red_roof_houses.py` imports building-type-a/b/c/d/e/f into a separate Blender process, identifies the green palette roof faces through their UV samples, and assigns a brick-red material. Walls and windows retain their original materials and UVs.

Original GLBs and textures are unchanged. Editable modified files are in `art/RedRoofHomes`; `roof-report.json` records the number of modified roof faces per model. Five houses are placed in the lakeside residential district and one in the civic district.
