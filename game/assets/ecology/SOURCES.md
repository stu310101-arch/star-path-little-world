# Botanical reserves — 2026-09-24

Original meshes authored for this project in Blender by `tools/author_ecology_blender.py`.
Editable masters are in `art/Ecology`; runtime GLBs remain separate from the original supplied asset packs.

- Alder: lobed, folded blades, branching trunk and root flare.
- Birch: light bark with narrow lenticel patches and individual leaf blades.
- Willow: descending twigs and narrow curved leaves.
- Pine: branch whorls with needle blades.
- Fern: nine pinnate fronds with individually modeled leaflets.
- Cattail: arching reed blades, stems and brown seed heads.
- Bank stones: continuous irregular weathered surfaces, vertex mineral strata.

These use actual leaf geometry and vertex colors, rather than primitive canopy balls. The four trees contain roughly 16,000 authored vertices each; instancing shares meshes across the world. They are stylized botanical assets, not photogrammetry scans.

No external downloaded textures or unlicensed models are introduced by this revision. Original sakura artwork and previous CC0 source packs retain their own provenance.
